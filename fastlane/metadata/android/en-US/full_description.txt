LiosTunnel routes your device's traffic through a server you control, using
Shadowsocks or SSH.

It is not a VPN subscription. There is no LiosTunnel network, no shared exit
and nothing to sign up for. You point it at a server you already run, and it
connects only there.

WHAT IT DOES

• Shadowsocks (aes-128-gcm, aes-256-gcm, chacha20-ietf-poly1305)
• SSH, with host key verification on by default
• All traffic through the tunnel, with DNS resolved through it too
• Live connection state, byte totals and current speed
• Keeps running when you switch apps or close the task

WHAT IT DOES NOT DO

• No accounts, no telemetry, no analytics, no advertising identifiers
• No traffic logging. Payload bytes and DNS names are never written to a log,
  by design and not by configuration
• No third-party SDKs
• Nothing leaves your device except the traffic you asked to be tunnelled, and
  that goes only to the server in your profile

Your server credentials stay on the device, in the app's private storage.

OPEN SOURCE

The whole client is open source, including the packet engine. If you want to
know what it does with your traffic, the answer is readable rather than
promised.
