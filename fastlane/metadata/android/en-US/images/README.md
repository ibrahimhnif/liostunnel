# Listing images

`icon.png` is generated from `assets/logo/liostunnel-1024.png` by
`packaging/make-icons.sh`'s source, at the 512x512 Play requires.

Still needed, and they have to be taken rather than generated:

- **Feature graphic**, 1024x500. Required.
- **Phone screenshots**, at least two, 16:9 or 9:16, min 320px on the short
  edge. Take them on a real device with a real profile connected — the
  Connection screen showing state, totals and live speed is the one that says
  what this app is.

Do not screenshot the emulator with a fabricated profile. The numbers on that
screen are the product, and a made-up one is a lie in a store listing.
